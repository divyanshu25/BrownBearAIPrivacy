Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads; it's made by one independent developer. If you use the AI editor with your own Claude or OpenAI account, that provider bills you directly. Apple Intelligence on the phone costs nothing.

Can it delete something I didn't mean to?

Every delete goes through Apple's own Photos confirmation and lands in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Nothing is deleted from your phone after a backup unless you turn that on, and then only photos whose every file has been verified in Dropbox.

Does it need iCloud Photos?

No. It works with the photos on your phone. With iCloud Photos on, it can fetch small previews to analyse photos that are only in iCloud; you turn that on in Settings.

Why Dropbox?

It's your account, with your files in plain folders you can open anywhere. BrownBearAI only ever sees its own folder. Other storage providers can follow.

Which devices?

iPhone and iPad on iOS 26 or later. Describing edits to the AI needs Apple Intelligence; everything else works on any iPhone or iPad running iOS 26.
