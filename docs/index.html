Questions

Why is it free?

It's made by one independent developer who wanted a photo app without a subscription. No in-app purchases, no ads, nothing to sign up for.

Can it delete something I didn't mean to?

Every delete goes through Apple's own Photos confirmation and lands in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Nothing is deleted from your phone after a backup unless you turn that on, and then only photos whose every file has been checked in Dropbox.

Will compressed videos look worse?

On a phone, they look the same to most people. Compress makes a 1080p copy, so a 4K original does lose detail you might see on a big TV. Your original stays until you choose to delete it. Keep the 4K ones you plan to watch on a big screen.

Does it need iCloud Photos?

No. It works with the photos and videos on your phone. With iCloud Photos on, it can fetch small previews to look at photos that are only in iCloud; you turn that on in Settings.

Which devices?

iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad on iOS 26 or later. Everything works on all of them. On iPhone Duo it uses the whole screen, closed or open, and folding it keeps your place.

Where does the music come from?

Ten soundtracks by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) come with the app, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. If you post a movie with one publicly, credit "Music by Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0". You can also use songs you've bought from the iTunes Store and downloaded to your phone. Apple Music streaming songs can't be put in a movie; the app tells you which ones.

Why Dropbox?

It's your account, with your files in plain folders you can open anywhere. BrownBearAI only ever sees its own folder. Other storage providers can follow.
